## Choose a Workbench workflow

- [Find my bug reports](workbench/FIND_BUG_REPORTS.md) — Narrate bugs and
  change requests, press one button, and hand a ready prompt to Claude Code or
  Codex; your agent opens issues only after you approve.
- [Built-in player](workbench/REVIEW_PLAYER.md) — Double-click a recording
  anywhere to watch it with its transcript, choose the audio track you hear,
  and mark a recording never-use.
- [Full Review](workbench/FULL_REVIEW.md) — Create the complete proxy, frame,
  transcript, translation, and review-package flow.
- [Transcript Only](workbench/TRANSCRIPT_ONLY.md) — Create searchable
  transcripts without a full media review.
- [AI / Ticket Evidence](workbench/CODEX_EVIDENCE.md) — Prepare local evidence and
  draft material from recorded observations.
- [Archive Catalog](workbench/ARCHIVE_CATALOG.md) — Build a searchable local
  catalog and archive-preparation indexes.
- [Review Media Inbox](workbench/REVIEW_NEW_VIDEOS.md) — Choose a bounded
  workload, process safe local work with one action, and hand the resulting
  queue to external Codex without moving files manually.
- [Raw bin library](workbench/RAW_BIN_LIBRARY.md) — See every recording in
  your recordings folders, what is in it, how much is idle, and mark what to
  keep.
- [Progress and Results](workbench/PROGRESS_RESULTS.md) — Understand active
  stages, terminal states, and completed outputs.
