Media Manglers v0.11.0 (beta)
=============================

Release status
--------------

v0.11.0 beta 1 follows v0.9.6 beta 1. v0.10.0 was prepared but never
published, so everything it held is in this release too. It is a beta: it
is published so it can be run on real recordings and refined from what is
found. It is the first public release; earlier builds are not available
here.

In short
--------

The Workbench is four features on one shared engine: Package any video for
AI, Find my bug reports, Watch the raw bin, and the AI Video Editor. They
find your voice, write it down and leave out invented lines the same way.

New since v0.9.6: a new look for every screen with a Text size setting; the
raw bin library; the AI Video Editor's own page, built around its player;
waveforms of every track in the built-in player; a History that keeps every
run; Find my bug reports' lists kept in the library; and a clear message
when a tool is missing. Every feature is described in one guide:
docs/workbench/FEATURE_GUIDE.md (also under Help in the app).

Read this before you upgrade
----------------------------

- The first time v0.11.0 starts, it moves your run history, Find my bug
  reports' never-use list, its list of recordings already done and its
  stopped run (the one Continue picks up) out of settings.json into the
  app's library. It also takes stray copies of those lists out of your own
  profiles. Built-in profiles are not touched, and nothing else in
  settings.json changes.
- Before it changes anything, it copies settings.json, your own profiles
  and the library into a backup folder and checks the copies. The backup is
  in %LOCALAPPDATA%\MediaManglers\Workbench\library\backups\migrations,
  with a HOW_TO_RESTORE.txt that says how to put everything back. These
  backups are never deleted by the app.
- If settings.json cannot be read, nothing is moved, a copy of it is kept,
  and the Workbench says so once and carries on.
- After that first start, do not go back to an older build. An older build
  does not know the history and the lists are now in the library: History
  would look empty, and Find my bug reports would offer recordings already
  done, and ones you marked never-use, as new. If you must go back, close
  the Workbench and follow HOW_TO_RESTORE.txt first; anything done after the
  move is not in that backup.

What a new PC needs
-------------------

The download holds the Workbench itself. Extract the whole zip and run
VideoManglerWorkbench.exe. To review recordings it also needs these, which
you install yourself:

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- Windows PowerShell, which is built into Windows.
- Python 3 (3.12 is the version the app suggests).
- FFmpeg, which includes FFprobe.
- Whisper for local transcripts, with PyTorch, both installed with pip into
  that Python. The Whisper model is fetched only after you approve it in
  Settings > Dependencies.
- Only if you use them: yt-dlp for web-video links in Full Review, and VLC
  for Play with > VLC.

Settings > Dependencies shows what is missing or out of date. Download opens
the tool's official page, and Copy winget command copies the install
command where Windows Package Manager has the tool. The app never downloads
or installs a tool by itself.

A new look (every screen)
-------------------------

- New dark and light colours, clearer text, rounded cards, a clear focus
  ring, and a bar on the selected row. Every drop-down list shows its arrow.
  Text is sharper at larger sizes.
- Text size (Settings > General / Startup): 100%, 110%, 125% or 150%. It
  changes the whole app at once and is remembered. Ctrl+Plus, Ctrl+Minus and
  Ctrl+0 change it on any page and in the player. Nothing is cut off at any
  size: rows wrap and pages scroll instead.
- The left menu is grouped as Make, Library, Results and Running, with
  Settings and Help at the bottom. Every button keeps its name, each has its
  own icon, and Progress shows a "1" while a job runs.
- Buttons say what they are doing ("Copying...", "Saving..."), cannot be
  pressed twice, then say "Copied" or "Saved" beside themselves, or what
  went wrong and what to do next. Save settings no longer opens a pop-up.
- A long job shows a banner on the page that started it, with a progress
  bar, the time left when known, and Open in Queue / Progress.

Finding your voice (every feature)
----------------------------------

- The track with your voice is chosen in this order, and the app says which
  decided it: track names saved by OBS ("from track name"); then the
  setting Settings > General / Startup > "Narration track, for recordings
  without track names", which gives Tracks 1 to 6 Auto, Narration, Never
  narration, Game audio or Full mix ("from your setting"); then listening
  ("auto-detected").
- A track named with "narration" is always your voice. Music, game, spotter,
  engineer, system, Discord and voice chat tracks never are.
- Full Review and Transcript Only use the same rule on Auto. With no voice
  track they transcribe the full mix, and never report "no narration found".
- Reading a long recording from a slow drive no longer times out. It stops
  only when it makes no progress for three minutes, and says how far it got.

Transcripts you can trust (every feature)
-----------------------------------------

- Words to spell right (Settings > General / Startup): one list of your own
  names and terms, given to Whisper by every feature. "Word=heard as" also
  corrects the transcript. It replaces the profile transcription glossary,
  keeps what you saved, and starts with iRacing, SimHub and Crew Chief.
- Lines Whisper invents are left out: stock phrases over silence or music,
  a phrase repeated over and over, lines heard on another track (music,
  game, spotter, voice chat), lines written while your microphone was almost
  silent, and guesses Whisper itself rated as unsure. Each is listed with its
  reason, and the built-in player shows it.
- The same recording now gives the same transcript every time it is run.

History keeps every run
-----------------------

- History keeps every run, not only the last 30. It shows the newest 200;
  Show more shows older ones. Home's Recent runs shows the newest five.
- Each run keeps what happened to every recording in it, as the feature
  reported it. A recording no feature reported on says "not reported".
- History says what really happened: a raw bin scan that could not reach
  the recordings drive is failed; a stopped Find my bug reports or AI Video
  Editor run keeps how far each recording got; an Inbox run shows your
  recordings folders; a New Review run, raw bin scan or Deep scan where
  some recordings failed is completed with problems; and a run cut off by a
  crash or by closing the app shows interrupted instead of vanishing.
- settings.json and your profiles are written safely, so a full disk or a
  crash never leaves half a file.

Find my bug reports
-------------------

- No microphone step: the app picks the track with your voice by itself.
- A ticket needs a request ("make a ticket", "open an issue", "instructions
  for AI", "feature request"). A word like "bug" or "fix this" said in
  passing is listed in MENTIONED_NOT_ASKED.md instead, and your agent is told
  to show you that list. Marker phrases match whole words only.
- A recording with no narration is a normal result: the run ends as Done.
- If the Whisper model is damaged, or the graphics card cannot be used, the
  page says so while the run is going, with the fix.
- About a quarter faster: the small review copy is made on the graphics card
  while Whisper works.
- Recordings are processed in the order you listed them. Continue leaves
  nothing stale behind, and Stop leaves no work files.
- The results say when the player's waveforms are ready.
- The never-use list, the list of recordings already done and a stopped run
  are now kept in the library (see "Read this before you upgrade"). Add
  newest, Include ones already done, Never use, Allow again and Continue
  work as before.
- A recording identical to one already done (the same content, in another
  folder) counts as done in Add newest. Never use stays with the copy you
  marked, and follows it when it is renamed or moved.
- A stopped run is no longer carried by Save profile as, by applying a
  profile, or by a settings export.

Package any video for AI (Full Review, Transcript Only)
-------------------------------------------------------

- Full Review makes its AI review ZIP by default. Every ZIP now keeps files
  in folders past the Windows path limit.
- Pause, Skip selected and Cancel review on Queue / Progress now work for
  Full Review, Transcript Only and Review Media Inbox runs, as for Find my
  bug reports. A skipped recording is not a failure.
- Transcript audio mode shows "Using main setting" or "Overridden".
- New Review: Preview track opens the built-in player on the chosen track
  at the Preview region. The Output step says why a folder is refused, right
  under the field.
- Notices that need no action (estimates, pauses, skips) no longer make a
  finished run look like it had problems.

Watch the raw bin (new: the raw bin library)
--------------------------------------------

- New Raw bin page (left menu, and a Home card): one lasting list of every
  recording in your recordings folders. Press Scan now.
- For each recording: the app's guess (Keep likely, Check, Probably
  nothing, Empty; Keep likely needs at least a minute of your speech), Idle
  time from the game sound (marked "estimated" when it came from the full
  mix), your decision (Keep, Delete candidate, Undecided) and labels.
  Decisions and labels follow a recording that is renamed or moved.
- The Mined column says which feature already mined a recording for
  tickets: Inbox, Bugs, Inbox + Bugs, Bugs: no narration, or Bugs (old list)
  for an entry from before the library kept the list, which is matched by
  its path. What Find my bug reports did is never in Export view.
- A different recording saved under a file name the library knows gets its
  own entry, Undecided and with no labels. The one that was there keeps its
  decision and labels and is shown as missing; put back, it gets them back.
  A different recording saved under a mined recording's file name is not
  shown as mined, except by an old-list entry, which only knew the path.
- Deep scan transcribes chosen recordings and counts your "flag for AI"
  requests. Export view saves the list as CSV or JSON. Play opens the
  recording at its first speech.
- Each recording is read at most once per scan and saved as soon as it is
  done, so an interruption loses at most the one in progress. The library
  is backed up daily.
- The app only marks. It never writes, moves or deletes anything on the
  drive that holds your recordings, and no feature may save its output
  there.
- Review Media Inbox is back on the left menu (Inbox) and on Home. It uses
  the shared voice detection and asks for the track only when that cannot
  tell; its track choice opens the built-in player on top of the dialog. A
  blank evidence or scratch folder uses the main setting.

AI Video Editor (now in the app)
--------------------------------

- Its own page: AI Editor in the left menu, and a Home card. It opens on
  Make a video from a recording, a Continue card for the video you worked
  on last, and Your videos: a picture card per video with a plain status
  (Ready to watch, Waiting for your AI agent, Exported, Getting ready...,
  Not prepared yet, Needs a preview). The page notices new versions by
  itself.
- Choose a recording lists the recordings the Raw bin knows, with what the
  app already knows about each: its guess, minutes of speech, idle time,
  your decision and labels, what mined it, and a video already made from
  it. Browse for another file... opens the Windows file picker.
- A video shows its versions as tabs and a row of labelled buttons: Video
  settings (Your voice, Game sound, AI budget, Privacy masks...), Prompt
  (Copy prompt for Claude Code or Codex, See the prompt first, Start a fresh
  edit), Story & review, and Files (This version's files, Render a preview,
  Export quality, the project and export folders, Refresh). A choice that
  cannot be used yet stays in sight, greyed out, and says why.
- The version's picture has its play choices on it: Play the final (full
  quality) with Play the preview (quick, smaller) beside it, or Play the
  preview when there is no final. It plays at once, on the page, in the
  app's built-in player.
- Under the player, the story is a strip of scenes. Click one to jump
  there; its pen adds it to your notes. Your notes and the scenes go into
  the next prompt.
- The AI budget box says what each budget changes. See the prompt first
  lets you edit the prompt for one copy. The story and the editor's own
  review open as a readable page, short summary first.
- Your AI agent starts with a plain-words plan of the story, reports in
  plain words, and ends with the exact next steps in the app. It no longer
  makes the final video itself: Approve and export... is your approval, and
  its dialog says it makes the video on this PC, levels the sound for
  upload, and uploads nothing.
- A video always edits its own recording. A different file, even one with
  the same name in another folder, is refused with the reason, unless it is
  an exact copy.
- Privacy masks: paint the parts of your cameras that must always be
  blurred. The AI editor's commands cannot remove a mask, or move, leave out
  or cover a masked camera. Prepare warns when no masks are drawn.
- Start a fresh edit: a separate edit of the same race, so a second AI
  editor can make its own cut.
- On the shared engine: the recording is read once; the voice and game
  tracks follow your track names and Narration track setting on Auto (a
  track you pick shows Overridden); your Whisper settings, words to spell
  right and the same invented-line filters are used, keeping short real
  reactions.
- Prepare also listens to the game sound for other voices (spotter, race
  control, other drivers), so the AI editor can check what is fit to share.
- Dead time: a minute or more where the game picture stands still or goes
  black, or the game sound is quiet. The AI editor is told to cut it unless
  it serves the story.
- Export at the recording's own frame rate, up to 60 frames a second,
  compressed once. Export quality: YouTube (recommended), Highest quality or
  Smaller file, with a per-video override.
- A revision that changes only the sound reuses the rendered pictures, so
  its preview takes seconds. Renders carry readable names with the AI
  editor's label.
- Renders stay under the loudness peak limit, and a finished render is
  written safely to disk before it is reported done.
- Settings > AI Video Editor: the edited-videos folder, the AI budget, the
  model names the prompts ask for, Export quality and Quick passes.

Built-in player
---------------

- A waveform lane for every audio track (up to eight), named with its role.
  Click a lane to go there, or its name to hear that track. Waveforms (W)
  shows or hides them.
- The sound is measured once in the background and kept in the cache
  folder; after a run, its recordings are measured at low priority. A run
  or closing the app stops it, and it carries on later.
- Next sound / Previous sound (Ctrl+Right / Ctrl+Left) on the track you hear.
- The moments the package found, marked on the time bar, with Next moment /
  Previous moment (] / [).
- It starts on the track the transcript came from, or the one a page asks
  for. Next speech / Previous speech (Page Down / Page Up) jump between your
  speech when a page hands it the stretches.
- It fits a 1366x768 laptop screen.
- Evidence: one [clips] row per recording, so clips and the small review
  copy can be played even from a package with no bug reports.

Start-up, tools and settings
----------------------------

- A missing tool is named in a bar at the top of the window, once per
  launch, with Download, Open Settings > Dependencies and Not now. In
  Settings > Dependencies, a missing or outdated tool has Download, and the
  selected row shows the official page and Copy winget command. VLC has its
  own row.
- The Workbench and both scripts look for each tool the same way: the path
  you chose, then PATH, then the tool's own install folders.
- Closing the Workbench during the start-up readiness check closes it at
  once.
- Results go to a "Media Mangler" folder in your Videos folder by default.
  A temporary folder is refused as an output folder, with the reason.
- Cache folder (Settings > General / Startup), with a size cap and Clear
  cache. Clearing it never loses a decision or a label.
- The Default profile list starts with None, so saving Settings no longer
  sets a default profile you did not choose.
- Licences: THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt names everything the package bundles,
  and the licence texts ship in a licenses folder.

Upgrading from v0.9.6
---------------------

- Settings, profiles and tool paths carry over. The profile transcription
  glossary moves to Words to spell right.
- The run history and Find my bug reports' lists move into the library at
  the first start, after a backup (see "Read this before you upgrade").
- If a saved output folder is a temporary folder, or is on the drive that
  holds your recordings, runs now refuse it: choose another folder. Nothing
  you already have is moved.

Known limits
------------

- Progress on long jobs: a Raw bin scan reports only when a whole recording
  is done, so on a very large recording it can look frozen for minutes
  while it works, Queue / Progress shows one row for the folder, and after
  Cancel review its count of finished items is wrong (272).
- If the library cannot be opened when the Workbench starts (a damaged or
  locked file), the Bugs page shows the never-use and already-done lists as
  empty for that session, and nothing on screen says why. Nothing is lost:
  they come back at the next start that opens the library (275).
- For people running the test suite: the parallel runner puts most screen
  tests in one process, so a full run takes about two hours instead of under
  one (273). The app itself is not affected.
- AI Video Editor: in some recordings the sound runs up to about one and a
  half seconds ahead of the picture, and the editor does not correct it yet
  (230).
- AI Video Editor: a replay or a moving menu looks like driving, so dead
  time there is not found. Its runs can be cancelled but not paused.
- Raw bin: Deep scan does not run the cross-track check, and does not give
  Whisper your words to spell right as a hint (233).
- Raw bin: idle time comes from the game sound only. The game-picture
  signal is used by the AI Video Editor only, and spotting whether someone
  is at the rig from a camera area is not built (217, signal 3).
- Raw bin: Delete candidate is only a mark. Deleting marked files is parked
  (218).
- Review Media Inbox keeps its own record of done recordings, apart from
  the library, for now (224).
- Next sound finds sound, not speech; on a microphone that also hears the
  game or a fan it can land a few seconds off.
- A full mix with no speech can still get invented lines (210).
- Find my bug reports keeps its package as a folder; an optional ZIP export
  is not built yet (204).

Housekeeping
------------

- Test texts and a code comment that held lines from real recordings now
  use made-up text.
- The full test suite can run in parallel, several times faster than one
  test after another.
- The build's start-up check and the tests use throwaway folders, so they
  never touch your own library, cache or privacy masks.
- The build's smoke launch fails only on a real hang, and a build refuses to
  start while the Workbench is running from its folder.
